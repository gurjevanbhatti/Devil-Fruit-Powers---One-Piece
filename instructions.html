HOW TO PLAY

  1. Press Turn on camera and get your face and shoulders in view.
  2. Pick what stretches with the buttons in the top right: Face, Arm or Fingers.
  3. Face: pinch your cheek, mouth or neck with your thumb and index finger and pull your hand away (or click and drag it). The dot turns red when you've got hold.
  4. Arm: hold an arm out straight. When the yellow ring fills, your arm shoots out like a Gum Gum Pistol. Drop it to bring it back.
  5. Fingers: pinch a fingertip with your other hand and pull. Just that finger stretches out like rubber.
  6. Let go and it snaps back with a rubbery bounce.
  7. Press Tracking (or the H key) to see where the camera thinks your hands are.
