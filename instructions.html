HOW TO PLAY

  1. Press Turn on camera and get your face and shoulders in view.
  2. Pinch your cheek, mouth, neck or arm with your thumb and index finger, then pull your hand away. The dot turns red when you've got hold.
  3. Or click and drag any part of yourself with the mouse (or your finger on a phone).
  4. Pinch a fingertip with your other hand and pull. Just that finger stretches out like rubber.
  5. Hold an arm out straight. When the yellow ring fills, your arm shoots out like a Gum Gum Pistol. Drop it to bring it back.
  6. Let go and your face or finger snaps back with a rubbery bounce.
  7. Press H to show your hand tracking, so you can see where the camera thinks your fingers are. Press it again to hide it.
